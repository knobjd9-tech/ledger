Sourdough Focaccia

8 servings · 990 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g sourdough starter
  • 390 g warm water
  • 15 g olive oil
  • 500 g unbleached bread flour
  • 10 g salt
  • olive oil (for coating dish)
  • seasonings (optional, e.g. onion powder, garlic powder, oregano, Italian seasoning)

Steps

  1. Mix together starter, warm water, and olive oil in a large bowl and stir for about 30 seconds.
  2. Add the unbleached bread flour and salt, then mix well using your hands or a dough whisk until fully incorporated.
  3. Cover the bowl and let the dough rest for 30 minutes.
  4. Perform 3 to 4 sets of stretch and folds every 30 minutes.
  5. Cover and let the dough sit on the counter for 12 to 15 hours for the bulk ferment until bubbly and doubled.
  6. Pour a generous amount of olive oil into a 9x13 inch baking dish, dump the dough in, and flip it around so it is coated in oil.
  7. Let the dough sit in the dish for another 2 to 3 hours to finish proofing and spread to the corners.
  8. Add desired toppings such as seasonings, pesto, cheese, and pepperoni.
  9. Dimple the dough using your fingers to create classic focaccia bubbles.
  10. Bake at 425°F for 25 to 30 minutes, then let cool for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
