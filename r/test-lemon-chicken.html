Lemon Chicken Pasta

4 servings · 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 12 oz penne pasta
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Steps

  1. Boil the penne in salted water until al dente, then drain.
  2. Season the chicken with salt and cook in olive oil over medium-high heat until done, about 6 minutes per side. Slice.
  3. Add garlic and lemon juice to the pan, toss with the pasta and chicken, and serve.
