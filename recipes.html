    What it does

    Google's Gemini AI reads cooking videos, photos of recipes (cookbook pages, recipe cards, screenshots) and recipe PDFs, then writes out the recipe with amounts, steps and estimated macros.

    • YouTube: paste the link. If the description has no recipe, AI watches it automatically.
    • TikTok / Instagram: save the video to your phone, then tap Scan photos or videos.
    • Cookbooks and recipe cards: tap Scan photos or videos and take a photo. Pick several to scan more than one page or recipe at once.

    Get your free key (2 minutes)

    1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Google account.
    2. Tap Create API key. If it asks for a project, pick the default or create one.
    3. Copy the key (it starts with AIza) and paste it below.
    The key is stored in your Google Sheet's script settings, not in this app. On Google's free plan, Google may use what you send to improve its products.
